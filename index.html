Quick answers about centers, passes, pauses, and nutrition.

What is the difference between a s.cultfit Center, an ELITE Gym, and a PRO Gym?

s.cultfit Centers focus on trainer-led group sessions, ELITE Gyms cover s.cultfit Centers plus top partner gyms, and PRO Gyms provide access to select partner gyms and group classes.

Can I see the vibe and crowd before I commit?

Yes, you can explore center schedules, check peak hours, and book trial sessions before picking a membership.

Can I pause or transfer my membership?

Membership pause and transfer options are available in the app depending on your active plan terms.

Do your plans include nutritional guidance to help me reach my goals?

Guided programs like Transform and Bootcamp include personalized nutrition support alongside habit tracking.
